# ScholarGuard - Product Overview

## Executive Summary

ScholarGuard is a commercial Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform that empowers educational institutions to uphold academic integrity through advanced technology. By combining plagiarism detection, AI-content analysis, and streamlined review workflows, ScholarGuard provides a comprehensive solution for modern academic assessment challenges.

## Product Vision

To become the leading academic integrity platform trusted by educational institutions worldwide, enabling educators to focus on teaching while maintaining the highest standards of academic honesty.

## Market Position

ScholarGuard operates in the rapidly growing academic integrity technology market, serving:
- K-12 school districts
- Higher education institutions
- Online learning platforms
- Corporate training programs
- Certification bodies

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Plagiarism Detection & Similarity Analysis

ScholarGuard employs sophisticated algorithms to compare student submissions against:
- Academic journals and publications
- Internet sources and websites
- Institutional repositories
- Previously submitted papers
- Commercial and open-access databases

**Key Metrics:**
- Similarity percentage scoring
- Source identification and highlighting
- Paraphrasing detection
- Citation analysis
- Cross-reference matching

### 2. AI-Generated Content Detection

As AI writing tools become more prevalent, ScholarGuard provides:
- Probability scoring for AI-generated content
- Pattern recognition for common AI writing characteristics
- Sentence-level analysis
- Consistency checking across submissions
- Historical comparison for individual students

**Detection Factors:**
- Perplexity and burstiness analysis
- Vocabulary diversity metrics
- Sentence structure patterns
- Transition word usage
- Stylometric fingerprinting

### 3. Teacher Review Workflow

Streamlined interface designed for educator efficiency:
- Batch submission review
- Side-by-side comparison tools
- Annotation and commenting system
- Rubric integration
- Grade passback to LMS
- Automated flagging for manual review

### 4. Administrative Oversight

Comprehensive tools for institutional management:
- Institution-wide analytics
- Department-level reporting
- User management and role assignment
- Policy configuration
- Usage statistics and trends
- Cost tracking and budget management
